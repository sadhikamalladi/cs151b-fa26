What you’ll learn

G1 Build a training system

Implement and debug a neural network: predictions, losses, gradients, and training.

G2 Understand design choices

Explain design choices, test their tradeoffs, and judge what experiments establish.

G3 Reason about AI in the real world

Evaluate AI safety, ethics, ownership, control, and governance in real-world use.

G4 Apply AI in professional practice

Build, use, and adapt AI for real needs; make and communicate sound deployment decisions.
