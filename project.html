Project Milestones & Deliverables

Improve a small language model under a fixed constraint. Your team chooses one of four missions. Each member proposes an algorithm and benchmark design, then the team selects an approach to build and present.

Key datesTeams

How the project works

  1. Individual write-up: each student proposes an algorithm and benchmark design for the team’s mission.
  2. Group submission: discuss the proposals, select one algorithm and one benchmark, and implement them using the starter code.
  3. Group presentation: present your approach and how it performed on different benchmarks.

After Milestone 2, staff will run every team’s algorithm against every other team’s benchmark in the same mission, plus a held-out staff benchmark. Results will be posted on a leaderboard. The top-ranked team in each mission earns bonus points.

Teams

  • Teams have 2 to 4 members. Submit your roster and mission choice using the team sign-up form by Monday, October 26.
  • Group contract (optional). We recommend a contract describing individual responsibilities, meeting frequency, and group deadlines. If you upload a signed contract with the sign-up form, staff will enforce it to help resolve conflicts. Without a contract, staff cannot mediate group disputes, and the group is responsible for ensuring project development goes smoothly.
  • Team changes. If a teammate drops the course or can no longer participate, notify staff with a private Piazza post. We will help you join another team.
  • Team issues. Report conflicts through a private Piazza post to instructors rather than in office hours, so staff have a written record.
