Deadlines will be stated at the top of each assignment.

Late homework loses 25% of its grade.

Quizzes test your understanding of the prior week’s homework. We drop the lowest quiz score.

Absences and extensions

Ordinary delays

Competing deadlines, travel, and routine technical problems follow the homework late policy and do not ordinarily qualify for penalty-free extensions. No permission is needed to submit within the acceptance window.

Illness and emergencies

If illness or a serious unexpected circumstance prevents you from completing work, request a penalty-free extension through a private Piazza message to Sadhika Malladi as early as possible. Identify the affected work, briefly explain the impact, and propose a new deadline. Showing partially completed work can help staff assess your request. Do not send diagnoses or medical records.

Staff will work with you on a reasonable timeline and may refer the case to other university offices as needed. Staff may also introduce additional extensions to address logistical or computing infrastructure issues.

Missed quizzes

Discussion attendance is mandatory. A missed quiz receives zero and is eligible for the one-score drop; no explanation is needed to use that drop. For further extenuating circumstances, send Sadhika Malladi a private Piazza message.

Academic integrity, collaboration, and AI

Do the work that helps you learn. Staff may spontaneously spot-check your understanding of a submission or its underlying concepts. The instructor takes academic integrity extremely seriously, with no lenience on violations. Suspected violations are handled through UC San Diego’s academic integrity process.

  • Ungraded assignments: All resources, collaboration, and AI are allowed. Try problems independently first to identify gaps in your preparation.
  • Programming assignments and homeworks: You may discuss concepts and debugging approaches and use AI assistance, but you must develop and verify your own submission. Do not exchange complete solutions or copy another student’s code. Follow assignment-specific restrictions on generated code, libraries, and outside resources. You are responsible for understanding and checking everything you submit.
  • Quizzes and midterm: Work individually, without collaboration, AI, internet access, or outside assistance.
  • Team project: Collaboration and AI-use policies for the design, experiments, and report will be announced when the project is released.

Submission, feedback, and regrades

Homeworks and programming assignments are submitted and graded through Gradescope.

Midterm regrade requests are allowed. If you request a regrade on one question, staff will regrade the entire midterm and adjust your score accordingly.
