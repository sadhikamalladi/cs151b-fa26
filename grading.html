How your grade is determined

Before any upward curve, your course grade is the lowest grade you earn across these four categories:

  • Programming assignments
  • Quizzes (best seven of eight)
  • Midterm
  • Course project, including the final presentation

Example: If you earn an A in programming assignments, quizzes, and the project, and an A− on the midterm, your course grade is A− before any upward curve.

For programming assignments, quizzes, and the midterm, A− is 90–<93%, A is 93–<97%, and A+ is 97–100%. The course project will have a detailed rubric for each deliverable. I may curve final course grades upward if needed.
