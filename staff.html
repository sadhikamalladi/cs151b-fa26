Contacting the course team

Use Piazza for most communication with course staff, including homework and programming assignment questions, concerns about missed quizzes, and grading questions. In most cases, message all instructors for the fastest response. Send sensitive concerns exclusively to Prof. Malladi.

All course accommodations proceed through the official UCSD protocol. Initiate the process as early as possible.
